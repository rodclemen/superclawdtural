# What's new in Superclawdtural

The changes you'd notice in the Superclawdtural universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## Coming in Clawd Clash 4.1.0

- **A new universe.** Superclawdtural joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds, Doctor Clawd, The Clawd Files, Stevie Clawdberg, Clawd of the Rings, Clawd Slayer and Stranger Clawds: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **79 characters from all fifteen seasons of Supernatural**, most with a signature move. Dean's cherry pie, Sam's laptop, Clawdstiel's wing shadows, Bobby's flask of holy water, Ellen racking her shotgun, Jo's knife in the bullseye, Garth's sock puppet Mr. Fizzles, Charlie crowned Queen of Moondoor, Rowena's hex bag and Death's chili dog. Facing them, Clawdley's snap and his hellhound's shadow, Azazel's yellow eyes, Meg's black ones, Abaddon's fist, Lucifer and the archangels, Demon Dean's karaoke, Dick Roman's Leviathan grin, Chuck's guitar, Gordon honing his machete and the monsters of the week. HEM turns up as a hunter with a sawn-off shotgun loaded with rock salt.
- **20 places**, from the Bunker's war room, Singer Salvage and Harvelle's Roadhouse to a motel room, the house in Lawrence, the crossroads, Stull Cemetery, Clawdley's Hell, Heaven, Purgatory, the Empty, the open road at night, Roosevelt Asylum, a roadside diner, Roman Enterprises, Apocalypse World, Moondoor, the orchard, Chuck's house and Jody's house in Sioux Falls, each with something moving in it.
- **Baby, the '67 Impala**, picks up whoever's done, the trunk swinging up on the arsenal as they go.
- **Back from Hell.** When Dean, Sam, Clawdstiel, Bobby or Jack compacts its context, hellhounds drag them under, and a moment later a fist punches back up out of the ground.
- **Clawdstiel has no sense of personal space**, Dean and Sam trade "Bitch." "Jerk.", the hunters practise painting devil's traps and the angels train with their blades. Abaddon chokes people from across the room.
- **Twelve transitions** from the show: The Road So Far, the Impala's headlights, a grave salted and burned, a devil's trap, demon smoke, angel light, hellhound claws, a cold spot, the Ghostfacers' camcorder, the motel's wallpaper, a Leviathan's grin and Chuck rewriting the page.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Dean and Michael, Sam and Lucifer, Clawdstiel and Metatron, Jack and Chuck, John and Azazel, Bobby and Clawdley…), and Death and Billie never lead two sessions back to back.
- **Clawd Royale**: Superclawdtural's cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, linked at the top of this page.
